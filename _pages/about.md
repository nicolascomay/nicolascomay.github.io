Hello!

I'm Nico, a psychologist and PhD in Neuroscience from the National University of Córdoba (Argentina). At the <a href="https://cognitivesciences.github.io" target="_blank">Cognitive Science Group</a> (part of the <a href="https://iipsi.psicologia.unc.edu.ar/" target="_blank">Psychological Research Institute</a>), my research focuses on the computational foundations of confidence in decision-making. 

Specifically, I combine behavioural experiments with computational modelling to characterise how confidence in decisions is generated and how it is used to guide behaviour in uncertain environments. I sometimes complement these approaches with neuroimaging techniques such as fMRI and EEG. 

More broadly, I’m interested in developing and testing computational models to better understand human cognition and behaviour. 

My doctoral work was generously supported by a <a href="https://www.conicet.gov.ar/about-the-conicet/?lan=en" target="_blank">CONICET</a> fellowship and supervised by Pablo Barttfeld and Guillermo Solovey.
